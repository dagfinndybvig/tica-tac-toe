Welcome to the Tic-Tac-Toe Deep Learning experiment!

This application uses deep learning and self-play to train an AI to play tic-tac-toe.

Features

  • Train a neural network through self-play (100 games)
  • Real-time training progress visualization
  • Play against the trained AI model
  • Trained model saved to disk for reuse
